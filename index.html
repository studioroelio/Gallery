studioroelio is the creative outlet of Roel Schiffers.

Roel creates paintings that reinterpret found moments from his daily life and travels. Often starting from his own photographs, he reimagines familiar scenes with vibrant colour, layered patterns, and a playful attention to the ordinary. His compositions feature flowers, interiors, objects of interest and shifting shadows, offering glimpses into his surroundings. A self-taught artist and full time architect, he lives and works in Switzerland.
